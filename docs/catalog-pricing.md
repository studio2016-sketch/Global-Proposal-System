# Catalog and pricing authority

The catalog separates what the system may recommend from what it may price.

A catalog item can be fixed, owner_quote, or site_survey.

- fixed: deterministic price exists in the approved catalog.
- owner_quote: the system may recommend the item but an owner-approved amount is required.
- site_survey: technical validation is required before a final commercial amount should be presented.

Historical rates, remembered prices, AI suggestions, website copy and previous proposals do not automatically become catalog authority.

Discounts, travel, custom arrangements, rigging, electrical work, venue labor, freight and unusual technical requirements should be represented as explicit commercial rules/items rather than hidden inside generated prose.
