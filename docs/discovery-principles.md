# Discovery principles

The public intake should feel like a private concierge, not an estimate calculator.

- Ask one high-value question at a time.
- Branch by brand and opportunity type.
- Collect enough information to recommend intelligently without forcing technical jargon on the prospect.
- Do not expose internal fit scores, pricing confidence, sales notes, or AI reasoning to the client.
- A budget answer informs discovery; it never authorizes pricing.
- Missing site facts create follow-up tasks rather than guessed specifications.
- Final technical architecture may require drawings, site survey, rigging/electrical review, or production advance.
- Every completed discovery becomes structured data that can feed an internal draft proposal.
