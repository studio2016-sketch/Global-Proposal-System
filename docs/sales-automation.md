# Sales automation

After owner-approved delivery, the system may automate administrative follow-up while preserving human commercial authority.

Signals can include proposal opened, repeat visit, section engagement, configuration changes, acceptance started, signature status and payment status.

Next-best-action rules should prefer restraint. A single open does not justify aggressive outreach. Configuration activity, return visits, incomplete acceptance, overdue signature and overdue payment are stronger operational signals.

Allowed automation: reminders, concise follow-up drafts, signature/payment status monitoring, scheduling a follow-up, CRM activity logging, fulfillment handoff after verified closing.

Not allowed without owner review: discounts, price changes, scope changes, promises of availability, contract exceptions, custom concessions, or replacing an approved commercial version.

Engagement analytics should be disclosed/implemented in accordance with applicable privacy and consent requirements before production tracking is enabled.
